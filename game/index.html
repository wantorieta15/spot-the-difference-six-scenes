How to play

Two pictures look the same — but the right one has sneaky shifts: things removed, recoloured, moved, resized, flipped or added. Click a difference on either picture to ring it. Wrong guesses cost 5 seconds. Find them all before the timer bar empties. Six scenes get denser and sneakier: a sunny picnic, a cozy library, a harbor at dusk, a snowy cabin, a lantern night market, and a space-station window.

Keyboard players: arrows move the crosshair, Enter guesses, Tab switches picture, H asks for a hint, L toggles the magnifier lens, N goes to the next scene, R restarts, and 1–6 jump straight to a scene. Hover any picture to zoom in with the magnifier.
